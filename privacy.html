Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

KickoffTime is an independent website that helps users convert sports event times to their local timezone and download calendar reminders.

Information We Collect

We may store your selected language, theme, and timezone in your browser using local storage so the site works better on future visits.

The current fixture tool does not require an account. If an optional reminder form is introduced later, this policy will be updated before personal information is collected.

Analytics and Advertising

We may use analytics tools to understand site usage and improve the service. We may also display advertising. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads.

Third-Party Services

The site may use third-party services for analytics, forms, sports data, fonts, images, and advertising. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings. If you receive email reminders, you can unsubscribe using the instructions in the email or contact us at contact@kickofftracker.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@kickofftracker.com.
