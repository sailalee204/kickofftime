Frequently asked questions

Are the times converted automatically?

Yes. The browser timezone on your device is used by default. Use the timezone menu above to check the same fixtures for another city or region.

Will a calendar event use the correct timezone?

Yes. Calendar files store the kickoff as UTC, allowing Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and other compatible apps to display it correctly wherever you are.

Will a followed team's calendar update?

Yes. Team subscriptions use a stable calendar address. When the verified schedule changes, the calendar feed at that address is rebuilt automatically.

Does KickoffTime provide live scores?

No. The site focuses on verified kickoff times, calendar tools, and basic completed results. It does not promise second-by-second live score coverage.
