About KickoffTime

KickoffTime helps football fans see verified match times in their own timezone. The site focuses on local kickoff times, calendar downloads, and clear schedule updates.

The project started with the FIFA World Cup 2026 and now follows the 2026/27 Champions League. Its purpose is deliberately narrow: make it easy to know exactly when a match begins, especially when European fixtures fall on the next calendar day elsewhere in the world.

What We Do

We convert scheduled match times into your selected local timezone and provide calendar files that can be imported into common calendar apps such as Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.

Independence

KickoffTime is an independent fan tool. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by UEFA, FIFA, any football association, or any club.
